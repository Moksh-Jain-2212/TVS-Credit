# Cash-Flow Forecast Evaluation

Generated by `scripts/add_cash_flow_forecast.py`.

## Method

The forecast uses pre-loan monthly net cash-flow history only. P10 is conservative, P50 is expected, and P90 is optimistic. Histories with fewer than 3 monthly observations return `insufficient_history`.

## Backtest

For loans with at least 4 months of pre-loan history, the final pre-loan month is held out and forecast from earlier months.
