1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

1.1 Project Overview

TVS NADI is an alternative-data underwriting system designed for borrowers who have little or no
traditional credit-bureau history.

Traditional credit systems often struggle with first-time borrowers because they rely heavily on historical
loan repayment data. When that history does not exist, uncertainty can be treated as risk.

NADI addresses this by analysing real financial behaviour such as:

        · transaction history;
        · cash inflows;
        · expenses;
        · account balances;
        · income stability;
        · recurring obligations;
        · cash-flow volatility;
        · financial resilience;
        · repayment behaviour.
The system does not collapse everything into a single generic credit score.

Instead, it separately measures:

      1. Risk
      2. Repayment Capacity
      3. Evidence Confidence
These three dimensions are then used to determine what credit decision can be responsibly made.

1.2 Core Product Vision

The core principle is:
          No credit history does not automatically mean high risk.

NADI attempts to distinguish:
   UNKNOWN RISK
   from
   HIGH RISK

This distinction creates an opportunity to lend responsibly to customers who would otherwise be rejected
because of insufficient traditional credit information.

1.3 Core Innovation -- SAFE_TO_LEARN

The most important NADI innovation is:

SAFE_TO_LEARN

Suppose a customer requests:
   1,00,000

Available alternative data suggests that the customer may eventually be able to support this amount.
However:

   Evidence Confidence = 58%
NADI may determine that 1 lakh cannot yet be justified with sufficient certainty.
Instead of immediately rejecting the customer, NADI calculates:

                                                                           2
   Current safe exposure = 30,000

The system can therefore recommend:

   SAFE_TO_LEARN
   Starter Exposure: 30,000

If the borrower later demonstrates successful repayment behaviour, this becomes new financial evidence.
NADI then performs the complete underwriting process again.
The safe exposure may become:

   30,000
          

   55,000
          

   75,000
          

   Potentially 1,00,000

Credit expansion is never automatic.
Every increase requires re-underwriting.

Module I -- Four-State Decision Engine

NADI supports:

APPROVE

SAFE_TO_LEARN

EVIDENCE_NEEDED

Not enough reliable information.

6. Risk Management & Mitigation
Risk 1 -- Data Leakage

Problem

Using post-loan transactions could allow the model to indirectly see the outcome it is supposed to predict.

Mitigation

Enforce:
   transaction_date < loan_date

for every underwriting feature.
Create explicit unit tests.

                                                                           29
Risk 2 -- Small / Historical Dataset

Problem

PKDD is useful for transaction modelling but does not perfectly represent modern Indian UPI/GST lending.

Mitigation

Clearly explain:
          PKDD is used to demonstrate and validate the core financial-behaviour methodology. Indian-
          specific integrations are mocked because production transaction data is not publicly
          available.

Do not pretend PKDD is Indian TVS customer data.

Risk 3 -- Unrealistic ML Performance

Problem

A very high AUC may result from leakage or problematic target features.

Mitigation

Investigate:
        · post-loan variables;
        · target-derived features;
        · train/test contamination;
        · overly obvious loan-status variables.

Prefer credible performance over impressive-looking fake results.

Risk 4 -- SAFE_TO_LEARN Appears Like Reckless
Lending

Problem

A judge may interpret the starter loan as "lend money just to test the borrower."

                                                                           30
Mitigation

Make the policy explicit:
          Information gain can only optimise among exposures that are already conservatively
          affordable.

The system never approves an unsafe starter loan purely to collect data.

Risk 5 -- Overbuilding

Problem

Too many features may prevent completion of the core innovation.

Mitigation

Prioritise:

   Risk
   Confidence
   Stress
   Repayment Envelope
   SAFE_TO_LEARN
   Re-underwriting

over:

   Chatbots
   Blockchain
   Complex fraud systems
   Cloud architecture
   Mobile apps

   No history
    uncertainty
    rejection

   No history
